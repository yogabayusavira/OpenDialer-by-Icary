# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

desktop (Tauri shell with a React webview)

## Stack

Vite, React, TypeScript, Tauri, and SIP.js.

## Users

Sales representatives and sales managers working through outbound calling queues on desktop, often with a CRM open throughout the day. Their immediate job is to progress conversations without losing context or accurate follow-up details.

## Product Purpose

Icary - OpenDialer is an open-source autodialer and CRM that brings calling, customer context, live conversation notes, and AI call assistance into one operational workspace.

## Positioning

The product keeps the active conversation, the contact record, and contextual AI assistance together while a representative works a calling queue.

## Operating Context

Desktop productivity software used during focused sales sessions. Familiar CRM navigation, contact records, queues, activities, and call controls are core operating patterns.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed concepts: autodialing, CRM contact information, Live Conversation, and AI call assistance. The first interface should be detailed without feeling overwhelming, work well at 150% zoom, and use familiar CRM patterns.

## Brand Commitments

The name is Icary - OpenDialer. The user requested a mostly neutral, light, restrained interface with clear hierarchy and the mature usability of established products such as HubSpot.

## Evidence on Hand

No customer claims, pricing, benchmarks, testimonials, production data, or visual assets were supplied. Interface entries and activity details are illustrative product content, not performance claims.

## Product Principles

- Keep the representative anchored in the active customer conversation.
- Make the next practical action evident without hiding needed context.
- Use familiar CRM conventions to reduce cognitive load.
- Let AI assist the work quietly rather than compete for attention.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use high-contrast text, visible keyboard focus, clear labels, and desktop-responsive layouts that remain usable at 150% zoom.
