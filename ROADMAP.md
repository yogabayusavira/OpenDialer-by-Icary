# Icary OpenDialer roadmap

This roadmap keeps the dialer focused today while documenting the places Icary can grow next.

## Current focus

- **Dial** — a focused calling workspace with the live conversation, AI assistance, call controls, and one-screen wrap-up.
- **My campaigns** — a user's view of campaigns they are working across their organizations.
- **Organizations** — users start in their **Personal** organization and can later switch between organizations.
- **Campaign structure** — campaigns organize **Offers**, **Lead lists**, and **Playbooks**. Lead lists will be the home for CSV lead uploads.

## MVP delivery order

1. **Dial** — finish the SIP connection handoff, real queue progression, saved outcomes, notes, and call history.
2. **Lead lists** — CSV import, field mapping, validation, deduplication, and assigning a list to a campaign.
3. **Campaigns** — create and edit campaigns, attach an Offer, Lead lists, and a Playbook, then start a queue.
4. **Offers and Playbooks** — create the sales context that guides each campaign and the conversation assistant.
5. **Home and My campaigns** — a rep's daily view of assigned work, recent calls, and resume-dialing action.
6. **Settings** — Profile, Numbers, and Integrations. SIP lives under Numbers; calendar, email, CRM, and AI connections live under Integrations.

These are the complete MVP surfaces. Call history, qualification criteria, call outcomes, and import history belong inside the relevant surfaces—not as additional sidebar items.

## Next up

- **Lead import and management** — CSV mapping, validation, duplicate handling, and list ownership inside Lead lists.
- **Insights** — one destination with both performance and activity views: calls placed, connects, conversations, meetings, and follow-up history.
- **Goals** — personal calling targets and progress toward them.
- **Organization home** — a lightweight organization dashboard.
- **Members and permissions** — invite teammates; today reps can edit campaigns, while future organization managers can control campaign setup and access.
- **Phone setup** — SIP connection setup first, with Twilio and other number providers available as additional connection options.
- **Integrations** — AI providers, Calendly and Cal.com booking, email, and CRM connections live in Settings rather than taking space in the main navigation.

## Later

- **Network** — a formal, non-prospect contact list for peers, cold callers, managers, and other professional relationships.
- **Job board** — an organization workspace for open roles and recruiting workflows.
- **Number operations** — number purchasing, assignment, health checks, and rotation policies.
- **Campaign automation** — richer qualification rules, disposition workflows, and routing.
- **Team coaching** — call review, scorecards, and shared playbook improvements.

## Navigation principle

The sidebar stays short because it is a rep's working surface, not a catalog of every feature. Setup-heavy areas such as phone providers and integrations belong in Settings; deferred work stays documented here until it earns a place in the daily workflow.
